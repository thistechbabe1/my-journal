# Project Sharon v2 Feature Ideas & Backlog

These features are frozen for Project Sharon v1 and deferred for v2 consideration:

## 1. Advanced Periodic Review Engine
- Multi-step guided weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual reflection wizards.
- Automated trend breakdown of mood, energy, completed tasks, and habit streaks over custom date ranges.
- AI-assisted draft summaries of wins and lessons learned (opt-in).

## 2. Multi-Year Horizon Planning (1y / 3y / 5y)
- Strategic horizon roadmap connecting 5-year visions to 1-year milestones and active seasonal chapters.
- Life area balance radar & energy allocation heatmaps.

## 3. Deep Memoir Trajectory Analysis
- Cross-referencing life timeline events with relationship interactions and career achievements over 5+ year spans.
- Exportable personal memoir book PDF generator.

## 4. Native Push & Mobile Notifications
- Multi-device Web Push notifications for immediate/high tier attention items.
